    03 about

    Rajiya Risha is a Bangladeshi Islamic vocalist whose nasheeds, hamd and naat carry remembrance into everyday life — quiet, sincere, and made to be returned to.

    Rooted in the Bengali tradition of devotional song and inspired by the wider world of Arabic and Urdu nasheed, her work blends clear diction, restrained arrangement and lyrics that mean something.

    From studio releases to live mahfils, the aim is always the same: to make remembrance beautiful, and to leave every listener a little closer to peace.
